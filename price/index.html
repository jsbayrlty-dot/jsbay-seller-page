Meet your broker

Javier A. Pagan

I'm the Broker/Owner of J & S Bay Realty, a seller-focused brokerage serving Pasco and Hillsborough counties. When you list with us, you're working directly with me — not a call center or a team of assistants.

I've spent more than 24 years in real estate and finance. Before opening my brokerage, I was a mortgage broker for 6 years and worked 6 years in banking at a major national bank, where I held Series 6, variable annuity and insurance licenses. That background means I look at your sale the way a lender does: your equity, your payoff, and what you'll actually walk away with.

I photograph and film every listing myself, price homes from current local sales data, and bring a builder's eye to getting your home ready for market. Whether you're upsizing, downsizing, relocating or settling an estate, my job is to make the process clear and get you the strongest result.
